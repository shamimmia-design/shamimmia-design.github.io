Featured Project — Complete Book

Word Search Puzzle Book for Adults

A complete paperback, designed end to end — cover, a 50-puzzle interior and a full solutions section. 104 pages at 8.5 × 11", delivered ready to upload to KDP.

The Challenge

Puzzle books live or die on readability. Grids must stay clear of the spine, difficulty has to progress evenly from puzzle 1 to puzzle 50 — and the cover has one job: be understood in a 3-second Amazon search scroll.

The Approach

A 15 × 15 grid system with generous, print-safe margins. All 600 words (50 puzzles × 12) verified in place, with a solutions section in matching order. The cover is bold and typographic — and tested at thumbnail size before anything was finalized. I still spot-check the grids by eye before delivery; software places the words, but a person should feel the difficulty curve.

Deliverables

  • Full-wrap cover PDF — spine calculated for 104 pages
  • Print-ready interior PDF, 8.5 × 11"
  • 50 themed puzzles + full solutions
  • Thumbnail-tested front cover

FAQ

Questions, answered

What do you need from me to start?

Your manuscript or puzzle content, your trim size, page count and paper type (white or cream) — plus 2–3 covers in your genre that you like. Don't have all of it yet? Start with what you have and I'll tell you exactly what's missing.

Are the files really print-ready?

Yes. Every file is built to KDP's exact print specifications: 300 DPI, correct bleed, safe margins, and spine width calculated for your exact page count and paper type. You receive print-ready PDFs you can upload as they are.

KDP rejected my cover. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Send me KDP's rejection email and your current file. Most rejections come down to spine width, bleed, margins or resolution — I'll identify the exact issue and rebuild the cover so it passes.

How long does a project take?

Typically 7 days for a cover, 10 days for an interior and 14 days for a complete book. Your timeline is confirmed with your quote before we start — no vague promises.

How do revisions work?

Every package includes revision rounds. You review each proof and tell me what to adjust; we keep refining until the book is right for print. If a direction isn't working, I'd rather restart it early than polish the wrong cover.

Do you only design books?

No. Books are my specialty and my main work for the last 6–7 years — but I started in general graphic design: logos, t-shirt graphics, business cards and flyers. I still take on select projects in those areas. If it prints, I can probably design it.

How do payments work?

Payment is via Payoneer or direct bank transfer. Once we agree on the project, I'll send you a secure Payoneer payment request, or share the bank transfer details with you privately — payment details are never published on this site. We always agree the full price and timeline before anything starts. No hidden fees, ever.

How will we communicate across time zones?

Mostly async — email or chat, with written updates at every stage — plus scheduled video calls when a project needs a real conversation. My working day overlaps UK & European hours and US East Coast mornings, and my average response time is about an hour.
